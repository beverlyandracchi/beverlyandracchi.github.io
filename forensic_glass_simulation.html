The Physics of Forensic Glass Analysis

Snell's Law

n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂. By measuring the angle of incidence and refraction, forensic scientists can calculate the exact refractive index of a glass fragment to identify its source.

The Becke Line

A bright halo observed near the border of a particle immersed in a liquid of a different refractive index. When the microscope focus is raised, the line moves toward the material with the higher RI.

Match Point

When the glass and the liquid have the same refractive index, the Becke line disappears and the glass becomes nearly invisible. This is called the Match Point.
